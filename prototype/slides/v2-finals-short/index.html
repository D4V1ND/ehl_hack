Appendix · not presented

The four questions we always get.

what was real?

Everything but the supplier base and the rate tables — those are seeded data, not code. The call you watched was placed on stage.

why a phone call?

Because suppliers answer phones, not APIs. Email and portal RFQ are the next channel behind the same answer sheet.

what stops a bad order?

There is no ordering tool. Live dialling needs an explicit opt-in, every call is forced to one demo number, and "unknown" is never guessed.

our ERP?

Read-only, one adapter class behind the interface two adapters already share — proven by the same test suite.
