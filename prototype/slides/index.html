The rules we are pitching under

From the kick-off deck (Munich Agentic Hackathon — Kick off).

timing

Sunday 12:30 — challenge pitches, three parallel sessions: 5 min pitch & demo, 3 min jury questions. Sunday 14:15 — final pitches with the hack council; finalists called on stage one by one.

hard rules

KISS — keep it short and simple. Only slides submitted on ehl.gg may be used, so export to PDF and submit before the deadline. Submissions that do not use Entire are rejected. Pitching rooms are announced on Discord; Notion is the single source of truth.

what the judges score — "building a convincing winning pitch"

1) The solution — break down your unique idea, what makes it fundamentally different. 2) The demo — show the product solving the problem, how would we use it. 3) The proof — metrics, results, benchmarks; why are you the best.

Each deck maps to that scoring order on purpose: solution before demo, demo before proof, vision and ask last.

how the time is spent

Q&A is 3 of the 8 minutes, so the backup slides matter as much as the pitch. Anything a judge might ask for and we cannot defend on stage — sizing above all — lives in the appendix, marked backup in the grid and costed at 0s.

Driving a deck

keys

→←space navigate · n speaker notes · o slide grid · t start/stop the countdown against the deck's time budget · r reset it · f fullscreen · p print · digits then enter to jump.

export for ehl.gg

p → Save as PDF, landscape, margins none, background graphics on. Every slide prints at exactly 1280×720; the presenter bar and notes never do.
